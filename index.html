Week 1

5 October 2026.

I'm politicus

Self-Discovery

Our first Seminar Kejuruteraan class began with Dr. Makhfudzah introducing herself. After that, each of us took a turn to introduce ourselves which was great to get to know the people I will be learning alongside through the semester.

Introvert personality

Self-Discovery

The first activity was self-discovery. We were given a QR code to scan and answered a set of questions. My result was Mediator. It describes me as introverted, intuitive, feeling, prospecting and assertive. It was an accurate description of me.I tend to be reflective and think deeply before I speak.

No Final paper

Course Introduction

Dr. Makhfudzah then gave an overview of the subject. She walked us through what we will learn from Week 1 to Week 14. She explains how we will be evaluated and how the marks are distributed. Knowing the roadmap this early helps me plan my time and be more prepared for the course..

Learning Journal / Week 2

Week 2

15 October 2026.

Seminar

Seminar

During Week 2, I served as the Master of Ceremonies for the seminar Engineering Seminar: The Future of Sustainable Energy held on 15 October 2026. The event brought together experts and participants from collaborating institutions. Stepping into the emcee role required me to bridge academic content with audience engagement and ensure smooth transitions.

\Seminar

Seminar

The technical sessions provided deep insights into clean energy innovations and modern digital engineering practices. Ir. Binyamin, S.T. M.T. Ph.D. presented Development Trends of Hydrogen Fuel Cell Vehicle Technology in Indonesia and South Korea. Following this, Ir. Galih Karnadhi M.Eng. ASEAN Eng. presented Measuring the Deep: Introduction to Measurement While Drilling and Digital Directional Drilling.

Seminar

seminar

Serving as the emcee significantly enhanced my professional development, particularly in public speaking, stage presence, and live audience management. Facilitating speaker introductions and guiding the transition into the Q&A sessions taught me how to keep attendees engaged during complex technical discussions while maintaining formal event protocols.
